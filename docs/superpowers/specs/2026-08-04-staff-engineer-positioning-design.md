# Staff Engineer and Fractional CTO Positioning

## Goal

Present Bryan as both a Staff-level engineer and a fractional CTO. Show technical depth, technical direction, and business impact in equal measure.

## Audience

The site should speak equally to:

- Companies hiring a Staff Engineer.
- Clients seeking a fractional CTO.

## Home page

Use this opening message:

> Staff engineer and fractional CTO who turns complex product and platform problems into reliable systems. I set technical direction, design cloud and event-driven architecture, and help teams deliver with confidence.

Keep all official titles in the CV unchanged.

## Projects page

Lead with outcome-based case studies. Each main case study should cover:

- The problem.
- Bryan's role and scope.
- Key technical decisions.
- Team or business impact.
- A system diagram.
- Measured results when verified results are available.

Combine Terminal Onboarding and Profile Manager into one payment terminal platform case study. Keep Arbitrage Trading, SoilMate, and Raket.ph as supporting projects.

Do not add private company names, confidential details, or invented results. Use anonymized descriptions where needed.

## Projects to document next

Recommend these themes for later case studies:

1. An engineering platform based on real shared services, automation, CI/CD, observability, or standards used across teams.
2. A fractional CTO engagement that shows product direction, architecture choices, team practices, and delivery outcomes.
3. A cloud platform modernization that shows migration decisions, reliability, cost, or delivery improvements.
4. A reliability or incident reduction program that shows work across systems and teams.

Only publish a full case study after its facts and results are confirmed.
